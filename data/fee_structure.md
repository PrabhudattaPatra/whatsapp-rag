## Hostel Refund / Withdrawal Policy

| Fee Type | Policy |
|---|---|
| Hostel Room Rent | Non-refundable once enrollment is complete and classes have commenced, even if hostel premises are not used. |
| Hostel Mess Fee | Refundable after deducting proportionate amount for months utilized + maintenance charges of ₹5,000/-. Outstanding dues adjusted before refund. |
| Withdrawal Before Classes | If hostel is booked but student withdraws before classes start, ₹10,000/- is non-refundable; rest is refunded. |
| New Admission Payment | 50% of hostel fee (Room Rent + Mess) required in advance; remaining 50% to be paid before reporting to hostel. |

## Scholarships

### Scholarship for Military Personnel
- **100% tuition fee exemption** for the girl child of personnel who lost their lives in service.
- **40% tuition fee concession** for the girl child of serving or retired military personnel.

### Scholarship for CBSE and CISCE Board Students
- **20% on tuition fees** for students scoring 95% or above in Class 12 exams.
- **10% on tuition fees** for students scoring 90% or above in Class 12 exams.
